# PlayCounter v1.4.3

Rate your games, fine-tune your history and count playtime beyond your PC.

### Your games, rated S to F

Give each game your own verdict: **S, A, B, C, D or F**. Choose **Tier** from a game's right-click menu, or set it in **Game details** next to the IGDB rating.

- Show your tier on game covers, or hide the letters through **Customize → Tiers on cards**.
- Sort your library by tier, with unrated games at the end.
- Filter by tier or choose **No tier** to find games you haven't rated yet.
- Save a tier filter as a shelf to keep your favorites together automatically.
- Try the new **Rate your games** guide with sample games; practice leaves your real library untouched.

### A timer for play away from your PC

Playing on a console? In **My Games**, right-click the game and choose **Playtime → Start timer**. Stop it from the same menu or from **Now Playing** to save a manual session in your history.

The timer keeps counting until you stop it, including across app restarts.

### Edit, split and combine sessions

You can now adjust saved sessions directly in **My History**:

- **Edit session:** correct the start, end or length. Set any two and the third fills in automatically. Scrollable time controls make small adjustments easier, with explanations when you reach a limit.
- **Split session:** turn one session into two. If you left a game running while you took a break, remove that time as part of the split. The preview shows the original session and both resulting parts.
- **Combine with…:** join two sessions of the same game with a side-by-side preview. The time between them counts as playtime in the combined session, and the preview shows that before you save.

Also fixes a stray separator before the **Manual** badge in History.

### Three more emulators

PlayCounter now tracks games in:

- **Azahar** — Nintendo 3DS
- **melonDS** — Nintendo DS
- **Xenia** — Xbox 360

Each game gets its own playtime, alongside the existing emulator support.

Update from **Settings → Updates** in PlayCounter, or use the Windows installer attached to this release.
