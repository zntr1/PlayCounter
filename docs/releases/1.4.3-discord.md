**PlayCounter 1.4.3 is out** 🎮

Your S-tier favorites, your console sessions, your history — a little more yours.

🏆 **Rate your games from S to F.** Show your ratings on covers, sort and filter by tier, or make an S-tier shelf that updates as you rate. The new **Rate your games** guide lets you try it with sample games.

⏱️ **Count console time, too.** Right-click a game → **Playtime → Start timer**, then stop it when you're done to save the session. It keeps counting until you stop it, even if you restart PlayCounter.

✂️ **Tidy up your history.** Edit session times, split a long session and cut out a break, or combine two sessions of the same game. A preview shows what you'll save, including any time between combined sessions.

🕹️ **Three more emulators:** Azahar (**3DS**), melonDS (**DS**) and Xenia (**Xbox 360**), with separate playtime for each game.

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.4.3 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.4.3).
