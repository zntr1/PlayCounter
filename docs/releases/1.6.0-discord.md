**PlayCounter 1.6.0 is out** 🎮

Your PlayStation games, how long a game takes, and My History for any dates.

🕹️ **Your PlayStation games, too.** Import your PS5 and PS4 games with the playtime Sony recorded. PlayCounter counts your console sessions live and shows your trophies. Right-click **My Games** in the sidebar → **Import from PlayStation**.

⏳ **How long to beat.** See how long a game takes, how far you are and how much is left. Mark it finished and add your time on HowLongToBeat with the values ready to copy. Open a game's details → **How long to beat**.

📅 **My History for any dates.** Step through months, weeks or days, or pick any dates, and see how it compares with the time before. **My History → Pick dates**.

✨ **New games up front.** Games you added in the last 7 days and haven't played yet wait in a row above My Games.

➕ **Several accounts per launcher** add up, status and shelves sit right in the right-click menu, achievements sort by date, rarity or name, the timer on your game uses less memory, and fixes.

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.6.0 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.6.0).
