**PlayCounter 1.2.2 is out: Epic Games import and a library that keeps itself up to date** 🎮

🟦 **Epic Games players:** import your Epic library. Sign in to bring in the playtime Epic recorded, or just scan the games installed on this PC. Sign-in runs in a private window, and your Epic playtime stays on your PC.

🔄 **Library sync:** after your first import, new games you install in Steam, Battle.net or Epic show up in My Games on their own. Uninstalled games lose their Play button, and reinstalled ones get it back without a re-import.

📁 **Games from anywhere:** add a folder like `C:\Games` under **Settings → Library import → Watched folders**. PlayCounter adds the games it recognizes, ready to play, and puts the rest in Discovered.

⌨️ **Keyboard:** arrow keys move through My Games, **Enter** or **Space** starts the game. No controller needed.

⚡ **Easier on your games:** animations pause while PlayCounter is in the background (no more lost FPS), and it uses less memory in the tray.

🧭 **New guides** for watched folders and the new imports, right from the release notes.

🧹 **Also:** right-click My Games for a quick import menu, an option to hide the tracking warning on cards, many more non-game apps ignored automatically (thanks for your ignore suggestions!), and fixes for guides and Battle.net installs.

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.2.2 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.2.2).
