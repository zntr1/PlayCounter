**PlayCounter 1.7.0 is out** 🎮

Screenshots on your sessions, counters for deaths and bosses, a pause button, and new guides for everything.

📸 **Screenshots on your sessions.** Press your screenshot key to save a picture of your game, no Steam or Game Bar needed. Add a caption right away and find each picture on its session in My History. Set the key under **Settings → While you play → Screenshots**.

💀 **Count deaths, bosses and more.** Each game gets its own counters. Count with a key, your controller or the + button, and see the numbers while you play, in My History and in **Game details → Counters**.

⏸️ **Pause a session.** Take a break without closing the game. The clock stops and counts on from the same number later, all in one session. Use the button on **Now Playing** or a hotkey.

🧭 **New guides.** **Help & guides** (the **?** in the title bar) has 10 new guides, and each step finishes once you've done it.

⚙️ **New Settings** with a menu, a search, and your own date format and 12/24-hour clock.

➕ **BlueStacks** games are tracked, shelves can hide their games, a **Quick add** hotkey, Shift + hover shows playtime per launcher, smoother imports, and fixes.

Update from **Settings → About**, or [download PlayCounter 1.7.0 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.7.0).
