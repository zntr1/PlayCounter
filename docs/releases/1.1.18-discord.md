**PlayCounter 1.1.18**

A small update to make organizing and importing your games easier:

- **Drag several games at once.** Select games in My Games, then drag the group onto Favorites or one of your shelves.
- **Fewer clicks when importing.** Steam and Xbox imports preselect the game file when there is only one suitable choice. You still review and confirm it. Import messages also make clear when a known match was linked or a file was sent for community review.
- **Choose what appears in popups.** Playthrough names are now off by default. Turn on **Playthrough names in popups** in Settings to show them; note text has a separate setting.
- **Better recovery when the server is busy.** A countdown shows the pause, background lookups retry automatically, and imports can wait and resume.
- **Clearer emulator badges.** Community matches, your own choices, and pending reviews are shown consistently across the app.
- **A clearer first visit.** The empty library explains automatic tracking and offers imports as an optional way to bring in past playtime.

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.1.18 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.1.18).
