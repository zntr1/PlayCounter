# PlayCounter v1.5.0

See your achievements, stay signed in to your launchers and close games right from PlayCounter.

### Your achievements, game by game

See what you unlocked on **Steam, Xbox, Epic Games and GOG**, when you got it and what's still missing.

Right-click a game in **My Games → Achievements**.

### Sign in once

PlayCounter keeps your **Xbox, Epic Games, GOG and Battle.net** sign-ins on this PC, encrypted by Windows, and shows all your launchers on one page: **Sidebar → System → Launcher accounts**.

### One import for every launcher

Steam, Xbox, Epic Games, GOG and Battle.net now import the same way. Your games come sorted, and **Wrong game?** fixes a bad match.

Right-click a launcher under **My Games → Import games**.

### Import from GOG

Bring in your GOG games with the playtime GOG recorded, start installed ones with **Play** and see their achievements.

### Stop a game right from My Games

A running game shows a red **Stop** where Play was. **Force stop** ends a game that won't close. On a controller: **X** (Xbox) or **Square** (PlayStation).

### A timer on your game

A small timer in a corner of the game's window shows how long you've played. A reminder can pop up every 30 minutes to 4 hours of play. Set it up in **Settings → While you play**.

### More

- **Fullscreen for your TV:** press **F11**, click the new button in the title bar or press **Menu/Options** on your controller.
- **Less CPU:** PlayCounter needs about a sixth of the CPU it used to while it watches for games, and the import review reacts faster.
- **Fewer messages at once:** at most two messages stack up in the corner, so they no longer cover the buttons you're clicking.

### Fixes

- Games with their own Java no longer count as Minecraft.
- Game files several games share are told apart by their folder.
- One game the server can't look up no longer stops an import.

Update from **Settings → Updates** in PlayCounter, or use the Windows installer attached to this release.
