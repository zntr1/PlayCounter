# PlayCounter v1.2.2

Epic Games import, a library that keeps up with your launchers, and watched folders for games from anywhere.

### For Epic Games players

- **Import your Epic Games library** from My Games. Choose **Sign in and find games** to bring in your account library with the playtime Epic recorded, or **Find installed games** to scan this PC without signing in.
- Sign-in happens directly with Epic in a private window that is cleared afterwards. PlayCounter never sees your password, and your Epic playtime stays on your PC.
- Games you never played start unchecked, so free giveaways do not flood your library.
- Epic Games gets its own tab in My Games.

### A library that keeps up with your launchers

- **New installs show up on their own.** After your first import from Steam, Battle.net or Epic Games, games you install there are added to My Games automatically, even before you play them. Games you removed are never added back. Turn this off with **Add new games automatically** in **Settings → Library import**. Xbox games still need an Xbox import.
- **Play buttons stay accurate.** When you uninstall a game in Steam, Xbox or Battle.net, its Play button goes away on its own. Reinstall it and Play comes back without importing again.
- A USB drive or network share that is not connected no longer erases your saved launch paths.
- Steam imports now also include installed games you have not played yet.
- **Optional:** turn on **Show Install in Steam** in **Settings → Game launching** to get an Install button for imported Steam games that are no longer installed.

### For games outside any launcher: watched folders

- Add a folder like `C:\Games` under **Watched folders** in **Settings → Library import**. Every folder inside it counts as one game.
- When PlayCounter is sure which game it is, the game is added and Play works right away, even before your first session.
- Anything unclear waits in **Discovered** and shows the folder it was found in. If you ignore it, the folder is dismissed. You can restore it in Settings.
- Watched folders need **Remember launch paths** and stay on this PC.

### Play from the keyboard

- The arrow keys move through My Games between cards and into the sidebar. **Enter** or **Space** starts the selected game.
- It works whenever game launching is on, no controller needed. **Escape** lets go of the selection.

### Quality of life

- **New guide** under **? Help**: "Watch folders for new games". The guides for importing and launching now also cover Epic Games, new installs and uninstalled games. Start them right from the release notes in the app.
- Right-click **My Games** in the sidebar to jump straight to the Steam, Xbox, Battle.net or Epic Games import.
- Don't need the warning sign on games without a known .exe? Turn off **Tracking warnings** under **Customize → Card details** in My Games.
- **Easier on your games:** PlayCounter pauses its animations while it is in the background, so it no longer costs FPS in games that share the GPU.
- **Less memory in the tray:** PlayCounter uses less memory while it waits in the tray, and the popup window only exists while a popup is shown.
- **Fewer non-games in Discovered:** many more everyday apps, like 7-Zip, Adobe tools, AnyDesk and Audacity, are now ignored automatically. Thanks to everyone who shares the apps they ignore.

### Fixes

- Pressing Play on an uninstalled Steam game no longer opens Steam's install dialog.
- Battle.net games no longer count as installed when an uninstall left their folder behind.
- Dialogs opened during a guide can be clicked again.
- A guide no longer starts the hotkey recorder when it reaches Keyboard shortcuts.

Update from **Settings → Updates** in PlayCounter, or use the Windows installer attached to this release.
