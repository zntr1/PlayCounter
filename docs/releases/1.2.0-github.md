# PlayCounter v1.2.0

A whole new look, Battle.net import, and a My History that tells the story of your playtime.

This is the biggest update so far. Every main view was redesigned, and there is something new for almost every kind of player. Pick your section below.

### For everyone: a new look

- **New design.** A graphite dark theme with amber accents, and a warm paper-style light theme. PlayCounter now has its own title bar with search, help, notifications, and feedback, plus a sidebar you can collapse.
- **Artwork everywhere.** A featured game fills a banner on top of My Games. Right-click any game and choose **Pin to banner** to pick it yourself. Now Playing uses the running game's artwork as its background.
- **Make it fit your screen.** Set **Content size** and **Menu size** separately in **Settings → Appearance**. The banner can switch between compact and full details from its menu.
- **Faster search.** Press **Ctrl+F** from any view to search your whole library.
- **Calmer notifications.** Toasts have a simpler design, and dismissed ones no longer get stuck on screen.

### For Battle.net players

- **Import your Battle.net games** from My Games. Scan the games installed on this PC, or choose **Sign in and find games** to also find the games on your account.
- Sign-in runs in a private window that is cleared afterwards. PlayCounter only reads your game list. Your login, account details, and CD keys are not stored or sent anywhere.
- Battle.net does not share past playtime. To include it, open the game and use **Adjust total playtime**. In World of Warcraft, `/played` shows your total.

### For stats fans: My History

- **A new hero at the top**: one big number for the time range you pick, and highlight cards for your longest session, best streak, most played game, busiest day, and comebacks. Click a highlight to jump to the sessions behind it.
- **Sessions** is a journal grouped by day, with markers for first sessions, personal bests, and "back after N days".
- **Insights** holds the chart (overview or detailed), the calendar, your play habits, and the activity heatmap.
- **Games** shows a new podium and a list of every game with sessions, average length, and last played.
- The title bar search filters your sessions while you are on My History.

### For achievement hunters

- Achievements open with your overall progress, what is next, and your latest unlock.
- Two tabs: **Milestones** and **Game ladders**. Each category is one row of steps. Hover a step to see when you unlocked it or how much is left.
- Earlier months fold away under the monthly ladder.

### For collectors and library organizers

- **Choose your own artwork from SteamGridDB.** Pick a banner for any game, and a cover for your own entries and tools. Right-click a game and choose **Cover and Banner**, or use the picture button on its card.
- **New game details.** A large artwork header with cover, badges, and IGDB rating. The **General** tab shows description, genres, playtime, and quick actions (Launch, Open on IGDB, Show in History). The **Files** tab lists the tracked game files and launcher imports.
- **New game journal.** Playthroughs are shown as cards with an **Add playthrough** button. Double-click a playthrough to make it active. Recorded sessions are shown in a table with a link to My History.
- **Customize** in My Games is sorted into Layout, Card details, and Library structure, with switches instead of checkboxes.
- **Launcher groups** in the sidebar are now optional.
- Simpler right-click menus, and shelf counts that match the active filters.

### For emulator players

- The Emulators view has a new header with your playtime and cards for the linked games.

### For players who fix detection

- **Clearer "Report wrong match" dialog** with the game's cover, choice cards, and a confirm step before you ignore a file.
- **"I'm not playing this right now"** in Now Playing ignores the file on this PC only and never reports it.

### For new players

- A larger start window and a simpler welcome.
- Steam, Xbox, and Battle.net imports appear as soon as your first game is tracked, even before you import anything.
- Guides under **? Help** are sorted into categories: Getting started, Your library, Playtime & stats, Detection & launching, and Settings & support.
- Closing the welcome no longer turns on **Share apps you ignore**. It stays off unless you turn it on in **Settings → Discovery**.

### Settings and control

- **Settings → Reset** has two new options. **Restore default settings** resets only your settings. **Start over** erases your library and history and restarts PlayCounter. Your backup files are always kept, so you can restore them later.
- If PlayCounter runs in the tray, the tray menu now shows when an update is ready.
- The **Update note** button in saved-session popups is now optional and off by default. Turn on **Update note in popups** in **Settings → Desktop popups**.

### Fixes

- The startup screen can be moved and has window controls.
- Opening PlayCounter from the tray, a hotkey, or a second launch while it is still starting now shows the window right away.
- Game cards can no longer be dragged while a dialog is open.
- Feedback replies no longer show up again after importing a backup.
- Now Playing stays focused on games that are actually running.
- Better name matching for games with special characters.

Update from **Settings → Updates** in PlayCounter, or use the Windows installer attached to this release.
