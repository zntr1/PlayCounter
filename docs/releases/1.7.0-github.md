# PlayCounter v1.7.0

Take screenshots of your games, count deaths and bosses, pause a session, and learn every feature with new guides.

### Screenshots on your sessions

Set a key under **Settings → While you play → Screenshots** and press it while you play: PlayCounter saves a picture of the game window to `Pictures\PlayCounter\<game>`, without Steam or Game Bar, and HDR games don't come out too bright. A second key, **Screenshot with caption**, opens a small box over the game to add a caption right away.

Each picture shows on the session it was taken in, in **My History** and in the game's journal. Open it to see it larger, change its caption, show it in its folder or move it to the Recycle Bin. The caption is stored in the picture file itself, so it stays with the picture when you copy the folder.

### Count deaths, bosses and more

Each game gets its own counters: **Deaths** to start with, then whatever you want to count. Count with a key of the counter's own, a controller combo (View/Create/Share + D-pad up) or the **-** and **+** buttons. Counts show on **Now Playing**, in the live timer, on each session in **My History**, and in the new **Counters** tab in game details, with a chart of the last 12 sessions.

### Pause a session

Take a break without closing the game. The clock stops and counts on from the same number later: still one session, saved without the paused time. Pause with the button next to the clock on **Now Playing**, on the live timer while your mouse rests on it, or with the new **Pause or resume timer** hotkey. The tray says **Paused**.

### New guides, all up to date

The **?** in the title bar opens **Help & guides**, with guides by topic and a search. There are 10 new ones: While you play, Correct your history, Achievements & trophies, Counters, Launcher accounts, Covers and banners, Add a game yourself, Custom games, Track software and Screenshots. Every other guide was rewritten for today's app. Each step finishes once you've done it, and **Show me how** starts a guide right where its feature lives.

### Settings, easier to find

Settings now have a menu with one group per page and a search that finds any setting. Hotkeys save as soon as you press them, and each feature's hotkey sits right under it on **While you play**. Under **General**, **Format region** writes dates and numbers the way your country does, apart from the app's language, and **Clock** picks 12- or 24-hour times.

### More

- **Shelves that hide games:** a shelf you fill yourself can hide its games from All games and search, the New games row, My History, Achievements and Now Playing. Pick the places under **Hide in** in the shelf's menu.
- **BlueStacks:** PlayCounter tracks the Android games you play in BlueStacks.
- **Quick add:** set **Settings → Keyboard shortcuts → Quick add game** to add a game from a small search box, even while a game is in front.
- **Playtime per launcher:** hold **Shift** over a card in My Games to see how much each launcher counted, or when you last played the game.
- **Card menu:** **Status**, **Tier** and the favorite item show what you picked, and **Shelves → New shelf** puts the game on a new shelf.
- **Banners:** upload or paste your own banner for any game.
- **Smoother imports:** a launcher game whose only suggestion has the same name goes straight to **Ready**. **Import** moves on to the next game at once, and the scan warning can be closed for good. Right-click a launcher in the sidebar to forget its imported data.
- **Tidier pages:** shelves that don't fit in one row fold into **+N more**, two or more emulators fold into one **Emulators** group in the sidebar, notices at the top can be closed, My History opens on the tab you used last, and an empty My Games offers **Add game**.

### Fixes

- Tier letters on covers show their tier colors, and the favorite star is amber.
- Game details keep one height when you switch tabs.
- A window restored from maximized fits on its screen again.
- Buttons such as the list view's Stop show the colors and sizes they were meant to have.
- World of Warcraft versions that share a file name (Wow.exe, WowClassic.exe) are told apart by their folder. If you play World of Warcraft, PlayCounter may ask once which game the file belongs to; your hours stay.
- Game ladders in Achievements load faster.

Update from **Settings → About** in PlayCounter, or use the Windows installer attached to this release.
