**PlayCounter 1.4.0 is out** 🎮

🌍 **PlayCounter now speaks your language:** Deutsch · Português (Brasil) · Español · 中文
🖥️ **Starts in your computer's language:** change it any time in Settings → General → Language
📦 **Installer, startup screen and What's new** are translated too
📅 **Dates and numbers** follow your language
⏱️ **My History → All time** now matches My Games, launcher playtime and your own edits included
🔍 **PlayCounter games without a file** get Match found, Level up all and Check for matches, just like launcher games
🙈 **Ignoring an app** no longer sends it to the community as a suggestion
🧹 **More Windows tools** like Defrag and the on-screen keyboard are ignored automatically

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.4.0 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.4.0).
