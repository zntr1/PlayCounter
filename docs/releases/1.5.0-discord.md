**PlayCounter 1.5.0 is out** 🎮

Achievements, launchers that remember you, and a Stop button for your games.

🏆 **Your achievements, game by game.** See what you unlocked on Steam, Xbox, Epic Games and GOG, when you got it and what's still missing. Right-click a game → **Achievements**.

🔑 **Sign in once.** Your Xbox, Epic Games, GOG and Battle.net sign-ins stay on this PC, encrypted by Windows. All launchers on one page: **System → Launcher accounts**.

📥 **One import for every launcher, now with GOG.** Steam, Xbox, Epic, GOG and Battle.net import the same way, games come sorted, and **Wrong game?** fixes a bad match. GOG games come with the playtime GOG recorded.

⏹️ **Stop a game from My Games.** A running game shows a red **Stop** where Play was, and **Force stop** ends one that won't close. On a controller: X or Square.

⏱️ **A timer on your game.** A small timer in a corner of the game's window, plus an optional reminder every 30 minutes to 4 hours of play.

📺 **Fullscreen for your TV** with F11 or Menu/Options on your controller, about **a sixth of the CPU** while watching for games, and fixes (no more Java games counting as Minecraft).

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.5.0 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.5.0).
