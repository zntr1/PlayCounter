**PlayCounter 1.2.0 is out: a whole new look** ✨

Our biggest update yet. Every main view was redesigned, so here is what is in it for you:

🎨 **Everyone:** a graphite dark theme with amber accents, a warm light theme, and game artwork across My Games and Now Playing. Set content and menu size separately in Settings. **Ctrl+F** searches your whole library.

⚔️ **Battle.net players:** import your Battle.net games. Scan this PC or sign in to find your account games. Battle.net does not share past playtime, so add it with **Adjust total playtime** (`/played` in WoW helps).

📊 **Stats fans:** My History is rebuilt. Pick a time range and see your longest session, best streak, and most played game at a glance, plus a session journal and an Insights tab.

🏆 **Achievement hunters:** a new overview with your progress, what is next, and your latest unlock. Milestones and game ladders are now easy-to-read rows.

🖼️ **Collectors:** choose your own banners and covers from SteamGridDB. Game details and the journal got a new layout too.

🕹️ **Emulator players:** a new Emulators view with your playtime and linked games.

🔍 **Wrong game detected?** A clearer report dialog, plus "I'm not playing this right now" in Now Playing. It only ignores the file on your PC and never reports it.

🆕 **New here?** A simpler welcome, imports that are easy to find, and help guides sorted into categories.

⚙️ **Also:** reset only your settings or start over (backups are always kept), an update hint in the tray menu, and an optional Update note button in popups.

Update from **Settings → Updates**, or [download PlayCounter 1.2.0 for Windows](https://github.com/zntr1/PlayCounter/releases/tag/v1.2.0).
